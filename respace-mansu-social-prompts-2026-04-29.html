Twelve copy-paste-ready prompts. Three per blog post (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook). Each one includes the brand voice rules, the visual direction, the final caption, and the hashtags. Mansu just runs them.

Read this once before running any prompt

Every Mansu prompt below assumes the brand rules in this block. If Mansu is generating an image, paste this brand block into the prompt before the post-specific direction.

  • Brand colors only: Midnight Navy #1A1A2E, reSpace Coral #E8604C, Warm Sand #F5EFE6, Pure White #FFFFFF. Coral is accent only, never a large background.
  • Typography: Headlines in Clash Display Bold or ExtraBold. Body in DM Sans Regular or Medium. No other typefaces.
  • Logo: "re" in coral, "Space" in navy on light or white on dark. Never recolored.
  • Photography style keywords (append to every image prompt): warm amber tones, golden hour lighting, architectural photography, shot on 35mm film, no text overlays, no watermarks.
  • People in images: 2 to 3 mixed-ethnicity friends, late 20s to early 30s, casual-professional wardrobe, candid joy. Never posed. Never looking at camera.
  • Voice: confident, irreverent, warm. No hedging. No corporate phrasing. Short punchy sentences.
  • NO em dashes anywhere. Use periods, commas, or colons instead.
  • Banned terms: never use co-living, deeded equity, ROI, returns, passive income, investment opportunity, or zero down to describe reSpace itself. Co-living and cohousing are only used in comparison or FAQ contexts.
  • Required terms: co-homeownership (self-description), membership interest (not deed), wet bar (not kitchen for suite), Assessment Fee (not Clarity Fee).

The financing piece is where most groups get stuck. Conventional mortgages do not lend to LLCs. Qualifying as individuals and transferring to the LLC after closing can trigger due on sale. We solved this by underwriting the LLC structure from day one. If you have a friend group that has been kitchen table planning a house purchase for a year, the math probably works. The structure is the part most groups miss. We have built reSpace to handle all of it. How to run these prompts

Each prompt block is self contained. Copy the full block (everything between the lines) and paste into Mansu. Mansu generates the visual asset. Mary takes the visual + caption and posts at the scheduled time. Disclaimer goes in the first comment, not the post body.

  1. Open the prompt for the post you are running.
  2. Click the prompt block to select all. Copy.
  3. Paste into Mansu. Run.
  4. Review output for brand compliance. Use the brand block at the top of this page as a final check.
  5. Save the asset. Drop it in the scheduling tool with the caption and hashtag set above.
  6. Schedule for the day and time on the post header.
  7. Post the disclaimer ("Not an investment. Not a solicitation.") as the first comment immediately after publish.
