Source 2 · respace.co

What is live on the production site

Direct inspection of respace.co on April 30, 2026. The state is clear, and worth being explicit about so the punch list lands right.

The blog itself does not exist yet

  • respace.co/blog → SPA fallback to homepage. No /blog index page.
  • All 4 canonical post URLs from the portal → SPA fallback to homepage. The pages are not live.
  • Footer "Blog" link → points to respace.co/#waitlist. Placeholder.
  • respace.co/sitemap.xml → SPA fallback. No real sitemap.
  • respace.co/robots.txt → SPA fallback. No real robots.
  • Zero JSON-LD schema anywhere on respace.co (homepage included).
  • OG images not uploaded. The /og/*.jpg paths the posts reference will 404 when shared.

What is live and useful for the blog when it ships

  • Persona-routed waitlist form on the homepage with data-persona-preset deep links (buyer-solo, buyer-group, broker, homeowner, investor, qualifier, property-analysis). The blog posts can plug into this with smarter CTAs than they currently do.
  • rsTrack() session telemetry that POSTs every pageview and CTA click to api.respace.co/track with a persistent localStorage session ID. If Jacob wires this into the blog template, every blog read becomes attributable to the session that later submits the waitlist.
  • The Grove subdomain (thegrovebyrespace.co) for property-specific deep links.

For Jacob

The punch list, ranked by ROI

Eight items. Items 1 through 3 are the unblock. 4 through 6 are the standard publishing infrastructure. 7 and 8 are the optimization passes that compound over time.

01
Drop the 4 HTML files at their canonical slugs
Option A in the portal docs. Each file is self-contained — inline CSS, schema, fonts — so no template work required to get them live. Highest ROI move on the list. Once shipped, the schema fires, the OG cards work (after step 3), the FAQ snippets become eligible, and the canonical tags resolve.
02
Stand up a real sitemap.xml and robots.txt
Sitemap lists the 4 post URLs plus the homepage. Robots references the sitemap. Without these, search engines cannot discover the posts efficiently.
03
Upload the 4 OG images to respace.co/og/
Filenames: realtor-feature-respace.jpg, cohousing-affordability.jpg, co-living-vs-ownership.jpg, co-buying-friends.jpg. Every social share is broken until this is done.
04
Build a /blog index page
Lists the 4 posts with title, eyebrow, hero image, and excerpt. Repoint the footer "Blog" link (currently #waitlist) to /blog. This becomes the canonical landing for organic blog traffic.
05
Run the GSC + Bing Webmaster protocol
8-step protocol documented in this portal and the strategy PDF. About 4 minutes per post. Confirms each post is indexable, schema parses in Rich Results Test, and pushes the URLs into the priority crawl queue. Bing in parallel because Bing powers ChatGPT search.
06
Wire rsTrack into the blog template
Same telemetry the homepage uses. Blog pageviews and CTA clicks attribute to the same session that later submits the waitlist. This is what makes content ROI provable.
07
Upgrade post CTAs to use data-persona-preset deep links
Replace generic #waitlist anchors with the homepage's preset routing. Post 04 (buy with friends) → data-persona-preset="buyer-group". Posts 02 and 03 → buyer-solo or qualifier. Post 01 → mixed. Routes blog readers straight into the right funnel branch.
08
Add the missing UI layer to each post
Visible breadcrumb trail (schema is already there), <time> published-date display, mid-article inline CTA card, related-posts rail at the bottom (link the other 3 posts), and a small social share row. Author byline can be "By the reSpace Team" until author pages are set up.
